Support

Email pipebooktheapp@gmail.com and a human answers — the same one who wrote the app.

Think a number is wrong? Say so first. Accuracy reports jump the line, every one gets investigated against the published standards, and the fix ships to everyone.

Common questions

  • Does it need signal? No. Everything works fully offline, forever. There is no sign-in and no license check.
  • Where do my starred sizes live? On your phone only. Nothing leaves your device.
  • What does the † mark mean? That value hasn't finished our dual-source verification yet. Verify against the spec before cutting.
  • How is the data verified? Every dimension is extracted from two independent published sources, machine-compared, and disagreements are hand-resolved against a third before shipping.
  • Can you add a table or calculator? Probably. Email what you need and where you'd normally look it up; requests from working fitters set the roadmap.

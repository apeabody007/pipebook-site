What's in it

Reference

  • Pipe schedules, NPS 1/8 to 48 — wall, ID in decimals and 16ths, weight per foot (B36.10M / B36.19M)
  • Fitting takeoffs — 90s and 45s, LR and SR, tees, reducers, caps, stub ends (B16.9)
  • Flanges, classes 150/300/600 — dims plus the full bolt-up: stud count, diameter, length RF and RTJ, ring numbers (B16.5 / B16.20)

Calculators

  • Rolling offset — set, roll, travel, run, and end-to-end cut length with real takeoffs and your welder's gap
  • Cut a fitting — any angle from a factory 90, with heel, centerline, and throat marks
  • Miter cuts — cut angle, cutback, segment lengths
  • Parallel offset — equal-spread runs through a turn
  • Feet & inches — running tape math that never loses a 16th

Where the numbers come from

Every dimension is extracted from two independent published sources, machine-compared, and hand-resolved against a third before it ships. The handful of values that haven't cleared that process are marked in the app, in plain sight, rather than quietly shipped.

If a number ever looks wrong to you, say so first. Accuracy reports jump the line, get checked against the standards, and the fix ships to everyone — usually the same day.
